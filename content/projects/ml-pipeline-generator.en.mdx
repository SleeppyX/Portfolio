## Overview

Users upload a dataset and the app walks them through a full machine learning pipeline:
cleaning, encoding, scaling, feature selection, training, and evaluation. A rule-based
reasoning engine recommends a choice at each step and explains why, so people without an
ML background can still understand how the final model was built.

Models are trained with AutoGluon, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, and scikit-learn.
This was a two-person team project.

## My part: data cleaning

- **Missing values:** fill per column with mean, median, mode, or forward fill, or drop rows,
  with only the strategies that fit each column's data type
- **Outliers:** detect with Z-Score or IQR depending on how skewed the column is, then clip
  or drop them
- **Duplicates and unused columns:** find and remove them in one click
- **Recommendations:** the rule engine suggests a strategy for every column, and
  "Apply All" or "Apply Selected" applies them in a single pass
- **Before/after view:** a summary table highlights how the data changed after cleaning
