## Overview

Users upload a dataset and the app walks them through a complete machine learning pipeline,
from cleaning the data to training and comparing models. A rule-based reasoning engine
recommends a choice at each step and explains why, so people without an ML background can
still understand how the final model was built.

This was our senior project for the Computer Engineering and AI degree at PIM. A friend and
I designed and built the whole project together.

## 1. Data Preparation

- **Upload:** CSV, XLSX, XLS, or JSON files, with a data preview and target column selection
- **Data cleaning:** data profiling, then dropping columns, removing duplicates, and handling
  missing values and outliers
- **Exploratory data analysis:** distributions, feature-vs-target relationships, and correlation
- **Data transformation:** encoding (one-hot, label, ordinal), scaling (log, standard, min-max,
  robust), and feature selection with drop recommendations

## 2. Model Process

- **Data splitting:** 80% training set, 20% test set
- **Model competition:** trains many models for classification or regression, such as random
  forest, logistic regression, SVM, kNN, gradient boosting, XGBoost, LightGBM, and CatBoost,
  with hyperparameter tuning
- **Evaluation:** a leaderboard on the training set, then accuracy, precision, recall, and F1
  for classification, or MSE, RMSE, and R² for regression, plus a confusion matrix

## 3. Insight

- **Feature importance:** permutation importance and the top 3 most important features
- **Data visualization:** per-column charts and a correlation heat map
- **Model guide:** the strengths, weaknesses, and evaluation score of the chosen model
- **Pipeline trace:** a step-by-step log of upload, cleaning, transformation, and modeling,
  with an explanation for each decision
