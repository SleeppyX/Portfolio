## Overview

E-CMIS is a front-end prototype of a case management system that I'm building during my
software developer internship. It covers one stage of the case process: reviewing cases,
routing them for approval, and putting them on the committee's meeting agenda.
The client is not named, and the screenshot has been anonymized.

## What I built

- More than 30 pages for different user roles, each with its own inbox and permissions
- Approval workflows with status tracking and deadline (SLA) alerts
- Document generation that fills Word templates and exports reports to Word, Excel, and PDF
- A statistics dashboard with charts and monthly report tables
- Optional Supabase backend, with mock data as a fallback so the demo works offline

## Tech

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, with Supabase for data storage.
